# Image prompts for Flashpoint

Use in Midjourney, DALL·E / ChatGPT, Ideogram (best for text) or Gemini. Avoid generating text inside images except with Ideogram; add the wordmark afterwards.

## 1 · App icon / logo mark (square)

> Minimal flat vector logo mark for a B2B sales-intelligence product called "Flashpoint". Three small dots converge from the left into one point that ignites into a short, sharp spark, like a flash point where separate signals become one. Geometric, clean, confident. Single accent colour deep burnt orange (#C2410C) on an off-white background, with a charcoal secondary (#1B1C1F). No gradients, no text, no 3D, no glow. Works at 32 px. Centered, generous padding, square format.

Variant for a dark background: *same, accent orange on charcoal #121315, spark slightly brighter #FB923C.*

## 2 · Hero / cover graphic (16:9, for the deck title slide or README banner)

> Editorial illustration, flat vector, 16:9. A calm off-white canvas with a faint map-like grid of a German city. Small dots (people) light up one by one inside three university buildings; in one building three dots connect with thin lines and a burnt-orange spark rises from them. Thin data lines lead from that spark to a clean e-mail card and a short ranked contact list on the right. Palette: off-white #F5F4F1, charcoal #1B1C1F, burnt orange #C2410C, one muted blue #3B5B7A. Precise, quiet, professional, lots of negative space, no text, no people's faces.

## 3 · "Signal scorecard" explainer graphic (16:9)

> Clean infographic, flat vector, 16:9. Eight horizontal signal bars on the left (each a small icon: people group, phone, calendar with arrow, graduation cap with plus, empty chair, clock, speech bubble, government building), each bar filled 0 to 3 segments in burnt orange. On the right one large circular gauge reading a score. Thin connecting lines from bars to gauge. Off-white background, charcoal lines, burnt orange accents, no text labels (they are added later).

## 4 · Ideogram version with wordmark (if you want text in the image)

> Logo for "Flashpoint" with the tagline "One sign-up is curiosity. Three is a flashpoint." Left: a mark of three dots converging into a spark, burnt orange #C2410C. Right: the word "Flashpoint" in a modern geometric sans-serif, charcoal #1B1C1F, tagline below in smaller grey. Flat vector, off-white background, horizontal lockup, crisp edges.
